## 📊 Why this exists

When journalists, researchers, or families ask basic questions like "what does a maid cost in Dubai in 2026?" — there's no single authoritative answer published anywhere. Government regulators publish minimum-wage policy but not market rates. Individual agencies publish their own rates but only for their own nationalities.

GCC Domestic aggregates verified data from all three sources (regulators, agencies, families) into one citable, annually-updated dataset. **Free to cite. Free to use. Free to fork.**
