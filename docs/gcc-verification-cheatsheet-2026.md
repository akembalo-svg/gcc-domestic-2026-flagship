# GCC Domestic Worker Hiring — Verification Cheat-Sheet 2026

One reference, six countries, five steps per country.

## 5-step pattern (same everywhere)

1. **Ask** for licence number.
2. **Verify** on regulator register.
3. **Call** regulator hotline.
4. **Cross-reference** on [gccdomestic.com](https://www.gccdomestic.com).
5. **Reject** cash-only / non-standard contracts.

## Big rule (all 6 countries)

**Never pay cash. Never wire to a personal account.**

Anyone who pushes for cash payment is the #1 red flag.
