# What is Musaned? (Saudi Arabia Domestic Worker Platform)

**Musaned** (مساند, "supportive" or "assisting") is Saudi Arabia's official electronic platform for hiring and managing domestic workers. Operated by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD), Musaned has been mandatory for all domestic worker contracts since 2016.

## ⚠️ 2026 WPS E-Salary Mandate

**From 1 January 2026**, all domestic worker salaries in Saudi Arabia must be paid electronically through the Wage Protection System (WPS) via Musaned. **Cash payment is no longer legal.**

If you hire a domestic worker in KSA in 2026, ask your office how they'll set up your WPS file — it's mandatory.

## How to verify an office (5 steps)

1. Search at **musaned.com.sa** with the licence number.
2. Confirm **"Active"** status.
3. Call HRSD on **19911**.
4. Cross-reference at [gccdomestic.com](https://www.gccdomestic.com).
5. Reject cash-only or off-platform deals.
