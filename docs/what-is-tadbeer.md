# What is Tadbeer? (UAE Domestic Worker Framework)

**Tadbeer** (تدبير, "arrangement") is the United Arab Emirates' government-licensed network of recruitment centres for domestic workers — maids, nannies, drivers, cooks, and caregivers. Operated by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE), Tadbeer was launched in January 2018 to replace unregulated private agencies.

## Snapshot

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Regulator | Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) |
| Year established | January 2018 |
| Legal basis | UAE Federal Law No. 10 of 2017 on Domestic Workers |
| Number of licensed centres (2026) | 240+ across all 7 emirates |
| Contract template | MOHRE-prescribed, bilingual (Arabic + English + worker's mother tongue) |
| Replacement warranty | 6 months, mandatory |
| Recruitment fees (typical) | AED 12,000–18,000 |
| Verification URL | [mohre.gov.ae](https://www.mohre.gov.ae) |
| Hotline | 600-590-000 |

## How to verify a centre is licensed (5 steps)

1. Ask for the **MOHRE facility number**.
2. Search at mohre.gov.ae → **Establishment Search**.
3. Confirm **"Active"** status.
4. Call MOHRE **600-590-000** to double-check (English/Arabic).
5. Cross-reference at [gccdomestic.com](https://www.gccdomestic.com).

If you can't complete step 1, walk away. If the centre pushes for cash payment, walk away.

## What changed in 2026

- Number of licensed centres expanded to **240+** across all 7 emirates.
- New Ethiopia–UAE bilateral wage floor (post-2025 agreement).
- More centres specialising in Philippine partnerships (Filipino maids = highest paid).
