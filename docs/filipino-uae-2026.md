# Filipino Maid Hiring Guide — UAE 2026

## Process

1. Use a UAE Tadbeer centre with a Philippine partner agency.
2. Sign the MOHRE-prescribed bilingual contract (Arabic + English + Tagalog).
3. Pay recruitment fee (typically AED 12,000–18,000).
4. Centre handles visa + medical + Emirates ID (4–8 weeks).
5. Worker arrives under 2-year contract sponsorship.

## Salary expectations 2026

- **Minimum AED 2,000/month** (DMW policy floor).
- All-in first-year cost: **AED 43,000–55,000**.
- Annual leave + return ticket home: mandatory under UAE Federal Law 10/2017.

## Key points

- POEA contract cross-check is helpful (ask for it).
- Confirm Hepatitis B + TB clearance from Manila (saves UAE medical time).
- Replacement warranty: 6 months minimum.
