# 2026 GCC Domestic Worker Salary Report (Summary)

The most comprehensive verified wage dataset for maids, nannies, cooks, and drivers across all six Gulf countries. Free to cite under CC BY 4.0.

## Highlights

- **Filipino salaries in UAE jumped 7%** to a median of AED 2,000/month.
- **Ethiopian salaries in Saudi rose 5%** following the 2025 Ethiopia-Saudi bilateral.
- **Musaned WPS e-salary mandate** went live on 1 January 2026.
- **Saudi-UAE gap narrowing**: median Filipino differential is now SAR 100 vs SAR 200 in 2024.
- **UAE Tadbeer expanded to 240+ centres**.

## Methodology

- Sample: 1,300+ verified MOHRE-Tadbeer + Musaned-licensed agencies.
- Time period: January–April 2026.
- Sources: Agency-published rates + family-reported actuals + regulator data.
- All figures = median, not mean.
