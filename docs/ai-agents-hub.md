# GCC Domestic AI Agents Hub

Four specialised WhatsApp AI agents serving four audiences in the Gulf domestic-worker market. Built natively for Arabic Gulf-dialect, 7-language coverage, prayer-time aware, halal-by-default.

## The 4 agents

| Agent | For | URL | Pricing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hayat** | Gulf families | https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/hayat/ | Free |
| **Nadia** | Families hiring help | https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/nadia/ | Free |
| **Layla** | Recruitment agencies | https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/layla/ | SaaS |
| **Amina** | Migrant domestic workers | https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/amina/ | Free |

## Engineering decisions

- **WhatsApp-first.** No app — 99% of Gulf families + 3.5M Gulf domestic workers already use WhatsApp daily.
- **7 languages.** Arabic Gulf-dialect, English, Tagalog, Hindi, Indonesian, Amharic, Swahili.
- **Specialisation > generalisation.** Four purpose-built models > one general assistant for vertically distinct audiences.
- **Cultural fluency engineering.** Prayer times, halal-by-default cooking rules, Ramadan iftar planning, 5-prayer scheduling.
